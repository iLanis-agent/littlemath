Little Math

A backlog of 50 with 8 arriving and 10 served per hour takes 25 hours to clear, not the 5 you get by ignoring arrivals.

Boundary: arithmetic only Little Math does steady-rate arithmetic on the numbers you type. It does not know how any real queue, line, team or machine behaves, and it gives no verdict on staffing, capacity or whether a plan will work. Every figure is your own entry.

The law truth

Solving for the items in the system: arrival rate 4 x time in the system 3 = 12.00. This is Little's law, L = rate x time, applied to your two numbers.

The drain truth

From a backlog of 50, service (10 per hour) beats arrivals (8 per hour), so the backlog shrinks by 2.00 per hour and is 44.00 at hour 3. It clears at hour 25.00.

The batch truth

A batch of 8 takes 26.00 min (3.25 min per item, 18.46 items per hour). No batch size can beat 30.00 items per hour. The smallest batch that reaches 20 items per hour is 10, which gives 20.00 items per hour.
